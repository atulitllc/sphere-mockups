Channel partnership

Stay in front of the client. Put AmiSoft behind the architecture.

Enablement for your sellers, co-sell when a pursuit needs it, and implementation kits your consultants can run inside the client’s quality system. You lead the account.

01— Enablement

Your sellers can describe the platform without inventing it.

Regulated buyers punish improvisation. Enablement gives pursuit teams a narrative, an architecture, and answers that stay inside what AmiSoft will actually implement.

  • A half-page architecture briefing for the people who will be in the room
  • A demo storyline for a clinical or R&D analytics scenario
  • Notes for the questions quality, security, and IT actually ask
  • A one-pager and slides your team can place under the client’s brand

02— Co-sell

The proposal should match the system you will stand up.

Co-sell is joint scoping, not a second sales team. We join before the estimate is locked, and we leave the commercial lead with you.

  • Scoping sessions that separate platform scope from your services scope
  • Assumptions we record so a proposal can be defended
  • Proposal language a quality reader will not have to unwind
  • Optional presence in the room when the client asks who stands behind the platform

03— Implementation kits

A kit your consultants can adapt, not a black-box install.

Delivery stays in your statement of work. The kit is the part that statement sometimes leaves thin: architecture, configuration, and the evidence pattern the quality unit will ask to see.

  • A reference architecture for a controlled scientific workspace
  • Configuration guidance for access, packages, and promotion paths
  • Qualification worksheets: requirements, traceability, and test evidence
  • A runbook for change control when the environment is updated

Responsibility

You lead the account. We make the stack implementable.

You hold

  • The client relationship and the commercial lead
  • Program management and the stakeholder map
  • Process design, change, and training
  • Fit to the client’s quality management system

We hold

  • Computing and data-platform design
  • Integration patterns and build support
  • Environment configuration guidance
  • Qualification evidence your team can adapt

The line we keep

Three commitments consultancies usually have to ask for.

No parallel motion

The client relationship stays with you. We do not open our own sales path into the account.

No false status

We will not describe the platform as validated, certified, or approved. Qualification is the client’s act.

No hopeful scope

If a pursuit sits outside what we can implement, we say so before it is written into your proposal.
