What you can implement

Platforms and services for regulated life sciences.

Each offering is meant to be delivered under your engagement, inside the client’s quality system. Nothing here is a certification, and nothing here replaces the regulated company’s decision to accept a system.

01 Data platforms 02 Computing environments 03 Integration 04 Qualification support

01

Data platforms & analytics workspaces

Clinical, quality, and R&D groups rarely lack data. They lack a place to use it without losing the story of where it came from. AmiSoft data platforms give your client governed zones, attributable access, and analysis workspaces that sit on those zones instead of copying everything into a shared folder.

Partners use this when a pursuit is about self-service analysis, a clinical data review space, or a quality data mart that must not be allowed to become a shadow database.

What you can take to the client

  • Separated zones for source data, curated data, and analysis
  • Role-based access with an attributable history of change
  • Workspaces for exploration that do not rewrite the source of record
  • A handover map your team can align to the client’s data catalog

02

Scientific computing environments

Scientific work needs runtimes: packages, jobs, notebooks, and pipelines. In a GxP setting those runtimes have to be definable. AmiSoft computing environments are controlled places to run that work — with package provenance, retained run context, and a clear line between exploration and a formal path.

This is the offering when scientists are still on unmanaged laptops, and quality is asking what, exactly, will be validated.

What you can take to the client

  • Declared runtimes and controlled package sets
  • Reproducible execution with retained run context
  • A formal path kept distinct from sandbox exploration
  • Environment qualification a validation lead can turn into protocols

03

Software & integration services

Most programs stall in the space between systems, not inside a single tool. AmiSoft integration services cover exchange among laboratory, clinical, quality, and enterprise platforms, scoped so your project plan has a delivery team.

We work from patterns for file- and API-based exchange. We do not promise that every instrument already has a connector. Where a system should stay the source of record, we say so.

What you can take to the client

  • File- and API-based exchange with controlled promotion
  • Explicit boundaries: what moves, what stays, who operates it
  • Estimates an integrator can staff and a client PMO can track
  • Handover notes for the team that will run the interface after go-live

04

Compliance & qualification support

A platform that cannot be explained to a quality unit will not go live. AmiSoft provides traceability, test-evidence patterns, and change-control language your consultants can adapt to the client’s own procedures.

Clients will still ask about 21 CFR Part 11 and EU Annex 11. The careful answer is specific: which records are electronic records, who can change them, and how that change is attributed. We help your team prepare that answer. We do not issue a Part 11 certificate, and we do not call the platform validated, certified, or approved.

What you can take to the client

  • User needs traced to configuration and test evidence
  • Worksheet patterns aligned to common CSV practice, including GAMP 5 where it helps the conversation
  • Change-control guidance when an environment or package set is updated
  • A written limit: the client’s quality unit accepts the system

This is delivery support for partner teams. It is not legal advice, nor a regulatory opinion, and not a substitute for the regulated company’s quality unit.
